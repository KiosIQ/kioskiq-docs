    02

    Take an order

    Category, dish, options, done. The ticket on the right updates as you go, so you can read it back to the guest before you charge.

    Anyone
    1. Tap the category — Juicy Burgers, Sides, Cold Coffee.Or type in the search bar at the top if you already know the dish.
    2. Tap the dish. If it has options, a window opens.
    3. Add any extras with the + buttons. The price at the bottom-left climbs as you add.
    4. Tap Add to ticket.
    5. Check the ticket on the right. The dish card now shows a small "1 in ticket" badge so you can see what you've already rung up.
    Fixing a mistake Wrong quantity? Use the – and + next to the line on the ticket. Take it to 0 and the line disappears. Wrong everything? Clear order.

    03

    Take money off a ticket

    Two ways: a percentage (10% off) or a fixed amount (£5 off). Both need a reason — that's what makes the discount traceable later.

    PIN if enabled
    1. With items on the ticket, tap + Discount.
    2. Choose Percentage or Fixed amount. The quick buttons change to match — 5–50% or £1–£10.
    3. Tap a quick amount, or type your own in the box above.
    4. Tap a reason. It fills in the note for you. You can type over it.
    5. Tap Apply discount. The ticket now shows the reason, the subtotal, the money off in red, and the new total.
    Which reason? Staff meal — team eating on shift. Customer compensation — something went wrong. Promotion — an advertised offer. Loyalty — a regular or a member. Pick the true one; these get reported.
    One thing the till can't do You can't discount a single item — only the whole ticket. If one dish is wrong, work out the amount and apply it as a fixed amount with the reason Customer compensation. Don't charge in full and refund it afterwards; that makes the day's figures wrong.

    04

    Take payment

    Charge asks two questions in order: where is the food going, then how are they paying. You can step back between them.

    Anyone
    1. Tap Charge £— at the bottom of the ticket.
    2. Pick the order type: Dine in, Takeaway, or Room service for a guest's room.
    3. Pick Cash, Card, or Pay Later.Got it wrong? ← Change order type takes you back a step.
    4. For cash, the change calculator opens. Tap what they handed you — £5, £10, £20 — or type the exact amount.
    5. Read Change to give and count it back. Then tap Charge.
    Room service Choosing Room service tells the kitchen this one is going upstairs, not to the counter. Take the room number in the customer name field before you charge.

    05

    Pay Later, and settling it

    Send food to the kitchen now, take the money when they arrive. The order sits in Orders with an amber warning until it's settled.

    Anyone
    1. Charge as normal but choose Pay Later. The order appears on the Orders tab, outlined in amber.
    2. The banner reads "Pay Later · payment still due." That's your reminder — the food can go out, the money hasn't come in.
    3. When they come to pay, tap Settle · Cash or Settle · Card on that order.
    4. Cash opens the same change calculator you already know.
    5. Once settled, the amber vanishes and the order turns Confirmed — a normal paid order.
    Before you go home Filter the Orders tab by Pay Later and check nothing is left unpaid. An unsettled Pay Later order will also block the Z Read at close, so it's better to find it at 5pm than at midnight.

    06

    Print and reprint receipts

    A receipt prints when the order is created. If the guest wants another — or the paper jammed — you can print it again any time from the Orders tab.

    Anyone
    1. Open the Orders tab on the right.
    2. Find the order. Newest sits at the top; use Search orders if the list is long.
    3. Tap 🖨 Print Receipt on that order's card.

    Older than today? Use Order history in the top toolbar and step back with ← Previous day, then print from there.

    If nothing comes out The sale is already recorded — the receipt is a separate step, so never charge the order again. Check the printer has paper and power, then tap Print Receipt on the Orders tab to try again. If it still won't print, write the total down for the guest and tell your manager; nothing is lost.

    The one you must not get wrong

    Refund or cancel?

    Ask yourself one question: has the money already been taken?

    Yes — they've paid

    Refund it

    Money has to go back out of the drawer or to their card. That's a refund, and it needs a manager.

    Go to Order history in the top toolbar. Full or partial. Task 07.
    No — nothing paid yet

    Cancel it

    No money has moved, so nothing needs returning. The order is simply called off.

    Also Order history — the order shows a red Cancel order button. Task 08.
    You can't pick the wrong one by accident The till only shows you the option that applies. A paid order offers Full refund and Partial refund, and no cancel button. An unpaid order offers Cancel order, and no refund buttons. If you can't find the button you expected, that's the till telling you the money situation is the other way round from what you assumed — check the Payment box, which reads PAID or UNPAID.
    07

    Refund a paid order

    Full refund returns everything. Partial refund returns chosen items, or an amount you type. Both need a manager at the till.

    Manager PIN
    1. Top toolbar → Order history. Pick the order from the list on the left.Not today's? Step back with ← Previous day.
    2. Tap Partial refund (or Full refund for the lot).
    3. Type a refund reason — say what actually happened, in a few words.
    4. Choose how many of each item to give back with – and +. Or type a custom amount instead.Watch the summary: refundable remaining, selected, and remaining after refund.
    5. Tap Confirm refund. A manager enters their PIN.
    6. For cash, the drawer opens. Count the money out.
    7. Type the exact amount you handed back. It has to match to the penny or the button stays greyed out. Tap Record.
    Discounts are already handled If the ticket had a discount, the till refunds what the guest actually paid, not the menu price. A £4.49 burger sold at 10% off refunds £4.04. Don't do the maths yourself — take the number the screen gives you.
    Why the exact-amount box exists It is deliberately fiddly. Typing the amount a second time is the check that stops the wrong sum leaving the drawer. If it won't accept your number, you've miscounted — recount, don't force it.

    08

    Cancel an unpaid order

    For orders where no money has changed hands — a Pay Later the guest never came back for, or a ticket rung up by mistake.

    Anyone
    1. Top toolbar → Order history.
    2. Select the order. Check the Payment box says UNPAID.
    3. Tap the red Cancel order.
    4. Confirm on "Are you sure you want to cancel order #—?" — Cancel order to go ahead, Keep order to back out.
    Read this before you use it Never cancel an order you have taken money for. If cash or card payment came in, it has to go back out as a refund, so that the drawer and the day's figures agree. Cancelling instead leaves the money unaccounted for, and it lands on whoever was on shift.
    Always tell your manager Tell your duty manager what you cancelled and why, especially for anything over a few pounds, and put a line on the shift sheet. It takes ten seconds and settles any question later.

    09

    Move an order along

    Every order walks through four stages. Tapping them keeps the kitchen screen, the counter and the guest in step.

    Anyone
    TapIt meansWhen
    ConfirmThe order is accepted and real.Straight away for kiosk and online orders you're happy to take. Till orders you charge are confirmed already.
    PrepareThe kitchen has started it.When it goes on the pass or the fryer.
    ReadyIt's made and waiting.When it's plated or bagged. This is what tells the counter to call the guest.
    CompleteHanded over. Done.When the guest walks away with it. Complete everything before close — open orders block the Z Read.
    Finding things fast The Orders tab filters along the top: by stage (Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Ready), by where it came from (Kiosk), and by Pay Later. Each shows a live count, so Pay Later 1 means exactly one order still owes you money.
    10

    Put a name on an order

    Every ticket starts as Walk-in Customer. Adding a name makes it callable, findable and — for Pay Later — chaseable.

    Anyone
    1. On the ticket, type into Customer name — a first name is enough at a counter. For Room service, put the room number here.
    2. Add a Customer phone if they're coming back later or it's a large takeaway.
    3. Change looks up an existing customer instead of typing a new one.
    4. The small icon beside it clears the customer and puts the ticket back to Walk-in.
    Always name a Pay Later A Pay Later order under "Walk-in Customer" is an unpaid bill with nobody's name on it. Take a name every time before you choose Pay Later.
    11

    Orders from the kiosk

    Self-service kiosk and online orders arrive in the same Orders list as the ones you ring up.

    Anyone
    1. The bell in the toolbar flags new kiosk orders as they land.
    2. Filter the Orders tab by Kiosk to see only those.
    3. They arrive Pending. Tap Confirm to accept, then move them along like any other order.
    4. Payment already shows on the card — CARD_ONLINE means it's paid, so don't take money again at the counter.
    If the list looks stale Tap ⟳ Refresh in the toolbar. It re-pulls orders from the server — worth doing at the start of every shift.
    12

    End of day: the Z Read

    Closes the trading day and prints the day's totals. Once you close, the day is shut — so it goes last, after the doors are locked.

    Manager PIN
    1. Toolbar → Z Read. Enter the manager PIN.
    2. Read the Blockers panel first. If it says Blocked, the day can't close yet — it lists exactly what's in the way, like "Active POS orders: 1".
    3. Go and clear them: Complete or Cancel every open order, and settle every Pay Later.
    4. Come back and tap Refresh. Status should turn from Blocked to ready.
    5. Print preview to check the figures on screen first.
    6. Print + close day. That's the day shut.
    Read the numbers, don't just print them Gross sales and Net sales are the day's takings. Discounts given is everything staff took off tickets. Cancelled value is orders that were called off. If discounts or cancelled value look high for the day you've had, say so before you close — it's much harder to look into afterwards.

    Reprint brings back a Z Read you've already done. History lists previous closes.

    13

    Awkward situations

    Things that happen on a busy shift, and what to do when the till doesn't have a button for them.

    Anyone
    What's happeningWhat to do
    Only one dish is wrong
    Cold, late, not what they ordered    		There's no per-item discount. Work out that item's price and apply it as a fixed amount discount with reason Customer compensation — before you charge. Never charge in full and refund after.
    They want to split the billThe till charges one ticket to one payment. Ring up separate tickets for each person, or take one payment and let them settle between themselves. Ask your manager before you start ringing up a large table.
    Guest asks about allergensThe options window shows what can be added, not allergens. Never guess. Ask the kitchen and check the allergen folder.
    No receipt came out after chargingThe order went through — printing is a separate step. Never ring it up again. Check paper and power, then reprint from the Orders tab. Tell your manager if the printer stays offline.
    Card machine declinesThe order is already in the till. Don't ring it again. Take cash instead, or leave it as Pay Later and settle when the card works.
    Guest walks off before paying a tabLeave the order open and tell your manager now, while you can still describe them. Don't cancel it — an open order is the record.
    You cancelled the wrong orderIt can't be undone. Ring the order again from scratch and tell your manager what happened.
    Order list looks out of dateTap ⟳ Refresh. If it's still wrong, the terminal may have lost connection — check with your manager before taking more payments.
    You need the drawer open with no saleThe cash drawer button in the toolbar opens it. Only for giving change or dropping cash — never to take money out.
